About the Role

As a Sales Agent at SOLE, you will be the key link between our artisan workshop and the design industry. Your mission is to establish partnerships with interior designers, architects, and commercial developers, showcasing our custom configuration capabilities and craftsmanship to secure high-value projects.

We seek a motivated self-starter who appreciates premium design and is eager to cultivate long-term industry relationships.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and contact interior designers, architects, and design studios.
  • Present SOLE's portfolio and bespoke configurator capabilities to prospective clients.
  • Consult on commercial and residential projects, preparing quotes and proposals.
  • Manage relationships with corporate clients and repeat design buyers.
  • Achieve quarterly sales targets while maintaining our brand's premium reputation.

Requirements

  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in sales, preferably in high-end furniture, design, or architecture.
  • Established network of architects and interior designers is highly preferred.
  • Excellent negotiation, presentation, and closing skills.
  • Deep appreciation for artisanal products and custom manufacturing.
  • High self-motivation and goal-oriented mindset.
